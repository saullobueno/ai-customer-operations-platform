# Reports, Tasks, Teams, Channels e Settings

## Reports

Métricas:

- volume
- backlog
- response time
- resolution time
- SLA
- CSAT/NPS
- sentiment
- AI resolution/deflection/cost

Filtros por período, team, agent, channel, product, customer e tag.
Exportação CSV/XLSX/PDF.
Scheduled reports por email.

## Tasks

Campos: title, description, assignee, due date, priority, status, ticket/customer.
Views: List, Kanban, Calendar.

## Teams

Support L1, Support L2, Billing, Technical, Customer Success.
Cada equipe possui membros, queues, SLA, business hours e assignment rules.

## Channels

### Email

SMTP/provider, endereços, assinatura.

### Web Chat

Widget, greeting, availability, routing.

### WhatsApp

Número, Business Account, templates.

### API

API keys, rate limits.

### Webhooks

ticket.created, ticket.updated, ticket.resolved, message.created, customer.created, sla.breached.
